Mizuho和Evercore将目标价提升至$650。Barclays下调曾压制股价；目前在AI情绪带动下反弹。

ANET — Arista Networks

AMD — Advanced Micro Devices

MRVL — Marvell Technology

AVGO — Broadcom

ADBE — Adobe Inc.
